# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Ruka.ai serves owners, operators, finance leads, and managers in restaurants, hotels, retail, and other inventory-heavy businesses. They arrive with operational pressure: purchase records, supplier payments, inventory, margins, and reporting are spread across manual work, spreadsheets, and disconnected systems.

## Product Purpose

Ruka.ai uses AI agents to automate operational and financial workflows for businesses with high inventory movement. The home page should make the value clear quickly, build trust, and move qualified visitors toward registration or a sales conversation.

## Brand Personality

Practical, intelligent, and reassuring. The brand should feel technically capable without becoming cold, modern without looking generic, and confident without overclaiming.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-purple landing pages, decorative dashboard mockups, excessive glassmorphism, vague automation promises, overstuffed feature grids, and startup language that could belong to any SaaS product. Avoid designs that feel like a template rather than a working business tool.

## Design Principles

Show operational relief, not abstract AI.

Make the conversion path obvious and calm.

Use motion to clarify and focus, not to decorate every section.

Preserve trust signals, pricing, guarantee, testimonials, and registration paths.

Keep the voice concrete, Spanish-first, and useful to a busy operator.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard accessibility. Respect reduced motion preferences. Keep copy scannable, forms readable, buttons obvious, and layouts stable across mobile and desktop.
