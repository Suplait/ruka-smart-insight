# Search and competitor research log

Observed 2026-10-08 for the initial Chilean commercial-intent scan. These sources establish that buyers actively search for purchase automation, document intake, invoice reconciliation and supplier-payment workflows. They do not prove Ruka supports every feature mentioned by another vendor.

## Initial interpretation

- The strongest commercial opportunities are concrete operational jobs rather than generic “AI automation” education.
- Registration and reconciliation SERPs contain software/ERP alternatives, so a winning Ruka page needs verified workflow detail rather than broad productivity claims.
- Supplier-payment results often explain the bank file or payroll-like batch itself. Ruka's useful differentiation is the verified upstream connection from approved invoices and receipts to the bank-ready file, while the customer retains final bank authorization.
- Integration content must distinguish “already implemented”, “technically connectable” and “requires evaluation” to avoid turning a logo into a guarantee.

Research is refreshed when a prioritized opportunity reaches implementation; this file is not a static keyword list.
