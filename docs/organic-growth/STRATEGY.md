# Ruka Organic Growth Strategy

## North Star

Increase qualified organic acquisition that contributes to commercial conversations, activations and new customers. Search visibility is an intermediate signal, not the outcome.

## Product lines

- **Ruka SaaS:** recurring operational workflows around purchases, documents, supplier information, costs, reconciliation, inventory and supported integrations.
- **Ruka One:** a commercial path for company-specific operational processes that span systems, rules and human handoffs.

The engine must keep both propositions distinct. A repository change is not a public product claim until its maturity and commercial availability are validated.

## Initial ICP and search territories

Priority starts with Chilean operations and finance teams that repeatedly move or reconcile information between SII, ERP, POS, banks, spreadsheets and accounting systems. Initial high-intent territories:

1. Automated supplier invoice intake and purchase registration.
2. Invoice, purchase-order and receipt reconciliation.
3. Bank-ready supplier payment files and accounts payable operations.
4. SII-to-ERP purchase information workflows.
5. Inventory, cost and margin updates across operational systems.
6. Company-specific back-office automation suitable for Ruka One.

These are hypotheses to validate against Search Console, public demand and verified product evidence, not a mandate to produce one page per phrase.

## When the blog is the right surface

Use a blog post when the query requires explanation, comparison, a workflow guide or education before a buyer is ready for a product page. Use or improve a product page when the intent is to evaluate a solution, capability or vendor. Do not create a post merely because a keyword exists.

A blog opportunity must satisfy all of these conditions:

1. Evidence identifies a real query, repeated buyer question or important supporting topic.
2. The post has a distinct intent and will not compete with an existing Ruka URL.
3. Ruka has a verified product or process advantage that can be linked naturally.
4. Claims can be supported by public Ruka evidence or clearly identified external sources.
5. The article can answer the query fully without filler, generic AI prose or manufactured statistics.

The default outcome is no post when these conditions are not met. Publishing cadence is not a success metric.

## Prioritization model

Every opportunity is scored from real evidence:

`priority = (2 × commercial intent + demand confidence + Ruka advantage − risk) ÷ effort`

Each input uses a documented 0–5 scale (effort starts at 1). The score orders work; it does not replace judgment about cannibalization, product maturity or conversion risk.

## Protected surfaces

Homepage, onboarding, pricing, commercial terms, critical forms, checkout and shared components affecting those surfaces require explicit approval. The engine records opportunities there but continues with safe work.

## Measurement

Use finalized Search Console windows (7/28/90 and reporting 15/30), public-marketing page segmentation, branded/non-branded distinction and experiment-specific evaluation dates. Do not infer leads or revenue from clicks.
